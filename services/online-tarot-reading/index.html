Frequently Asked Questions

What can a tarot reading with Akankshaa help me with?

Sessions can help you explore love and relationships, career and finances, family dynamics, and other important life decisions. The cards offer perspective and clarity — they are a tool for reflection, not a guarantee of specific outcomes.

What's the difference between an audio and a phone tarot reading?

An audio reading is a recorded session delivered to you to listen to at your own pace, prepared based on the questions you submit when booking. A phone reading is a live, real-time consultation where you can ask follow-up questions as the session unfolds.

How long does it take to receive my reading?

Delivery time depends on the option you choose at booking and is confirmed on the booking page before checkout.

Is tarot a substitute for professional advice?

No. Tarot offers spiritual guidance and perspective. It does not replace medical, legal, psychological, or financial professionals, and Akankshaa does not make guarantees about specific outcomes.
