How Ritual Requests Work

You'll describe your intention and situation when requesting a ritual. Akankshaa reviews your request and designs bespoke ritual work aligned with your specific goal, performed with care and positive intention.

Ethics and Responsible Practice

All ritual work is performed ethically, respecting the free will of everyone involved. Akankshaa does not perform rituals intended to harm others or override another person's consent.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of spiritual rituals does The Ocultt Tarot offer?

Rituals are intentional spiritual work performed with positive energy to support areas such as love, abundance, protection, confidence, career growth, and personal transformation.

How is ritual work personalized?

You'll share your intention and situation when booking, and Akankshaa designs the ritual around your specific goal rather than using a generic template.

Are outcomes guaranteed?

No. Ritual work is offered as intentional spiritual support. Akankshaa does not guarantee specific outcomes about relationships, health, money, or other life circumstances, and always respects free will and ethical practice.
